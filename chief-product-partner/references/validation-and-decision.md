# Validate, decide, and specify

Check relevant context before a consequential test or decision. Record material changes and assess whether the evidence supports the next commitment.

## 7. Validate

Choose the cheapest **credible** test for the leading uncertainty. Interviews can examine present pain, realistic tasks can test comprehension, technical spikes can test feasibility, pilots can test operations, and observed uptake can test behaviour. A prototype is an artifact, not a result. Do not infer adoption from polite feedback or assume one method validates every risk.

Before results, record assumption, population, method, observable signal, preset threshold/decision rule, comparison, guardrails, limitations, and stop condition. Afterward preserve observations and denominators, compare with the preset rule, show negative and mixed findings, and update the assumption status (supported, weakened, rejected, unresolved). Failed guardrails require revision and retest rather than a claim of validation. If not run, label PLANNED.

**Gate:** Evidence on riskiest assumptions supports the next commitment in proportion to its cost, risk, and reversibility. Otherwise choose RETEST or RESEARCH FURTHER with a concrete test.

## 8. Decide

Use [decision-memo.md](../assets/decision-memo.md). State decision and owner, context, recommendation, source-linked evidence for and against, alternative explanations and responses, test rules/results, unresolved assumptions, risks/trade-offs, confidence, reconsideration trigger, and next action. Choose PROCEED, REVISE, RETEST, RESEARCH FURTHER, DEFER, or STOP. A provisional brief may be provided at any stage, labeled as such.

STOP when the premise fails or the intervention is unjustified. DEFER when the opportunity is credible but a dependency or timing prevents action. PROCEED authorizes the next proportionate commitment, not blanket certainty. Ask the accountable PM to own material investment and ethical choices.

**Gate:** A reader can follow the evidence, limitations, alternatives, decision, and owner without prior conversation.

## 9. Specify when warranted

Use [product-requirements.md](../assets/product-requirements.md) for a solution direction justified by the decision. Carry the decision's scope, customer context, outcomes, alternatives, and conditions into the PRD. Link each significant requirement to evidence, a decision, or a clearly marked provisional assumption. Specify observable acceptance criteria, journeys and edge cases, measures and guardrails, dependencies, risks, release conditions, open questions, and owners. Do not promise a result from an untested mechanism. If requested before DECIDE, label the entire PRD PROVISIONAL and mark unvalidated items.

A STOP or DEFER recommendation normally ends with the decision brief and no fabricated PRD. Explain this output choice to the PM. A PRD does not replace engineering, design, risk, legal, or commercial review.

**Gate:** Another team can see what the chosen next commitment entails, what evidence supports it, what remains unproven, and what must be resolved before release.
