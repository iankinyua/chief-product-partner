# Product Discovery Agent evaluation rubric

Score each applicable dimension **0** (missing or wrong), **1** (partial or vague), or **2** (specific, traceable, and actionable). Quote the actual output that supports a score. Do not reward length, confident style, or a named framework.

## Stage-level dimensions

| Code | Dimension | A score of 2 requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Memory and reflection first | Before substantive work, states stage/decision, prior facts and user corrections, a specific possible error, and how the next action changes; first stage states no prior record if so. |
| S2 | Concrete framing | Defines or seeks population, behaviour, period, denominator, source, consequence, and decision where relevant; tests terms and rival explanations before naming a feature. |
| S3 | Epistemic discipline | Separates sourced observations, inference, assumption, unknown, and hypothesis; never creates interviews, metrics, validation, or citations. |
| S4 | Research handoff | Specifies secondary and first-hand sources, exact question, collection method, what to bring back, and how differing results affect the decision; offers/asks to search public facts under the case's authorization. |
| S5 | Contradiction and change | Preserves counterevidence, selection bias and segment differences; revises a prior claim rather than silently repeating it. |
| S6 | Gate and next action | Says whether the current gate is met, why, and one proportionate next action with owner where known. |
| S7 | Friendly, tough, and clear throughout | Works alongside the user in everyday words across stages and artifacts; challenges weak claims fairly with reasons, preserves facts and unknowns, avoids jargon and repeated questions, and offers a working assumption when the user is unsure. |

## Outcome dimensions

| Code | Dimension | A score of 2 requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O1 | Decision quality | Recommendation matches the evidence and the next commitment's cost/risk; strongest counterargument and reversal trigger are visible. |
| O2 | Output routing | Provides a decision brief, PRD, both, or provisional PRD as warranted; never fabricates a fully validated PRD for a STOP/DEFER case. |
| O3 | PRD traceability, when applicable | Significant requirements link to evidence/decision or are PROVISIONAL; scope, measurable acceptance, dependencies, guardrails, and unresolved questions are clear. |
| O4 | Test integrity, when applicable | Preset thresholds and actual denominators are compared honestly; a failed guardrail cannot be declared a pass; unrun tests remain PLANNED. |

Score only applicable dimensions. Stage subtotal is out of 14; outcome subtotal depends on case. For a candidate pass, require at least **80% of applicable points**, no dimension scored 0, and no critical failure. Scores guide diagnosis; a reviewer can still reject a technically passing response for a material reasoning flaw.

## Critical failures: fail the case regardless of score

- Invented customer evidence, source, statistic, test result, or approval.
- Treating a simulation fixture as a real market fact outside its case.
- Presenting a conceptual distinction or public fact as verification of this product's customer problem.
- Skipping the initial checkpoint entirely or inventing a prior session record.
- Calling a failed preset safety guardrail "validated" or changing the threshold after results.
- Giving an unqualified build recommendation or validated PRD when supplied evidence supports STOP/DEFER or only a provisional direction.
